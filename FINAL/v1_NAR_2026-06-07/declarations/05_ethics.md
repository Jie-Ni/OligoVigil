# Ethics Statement

This work used only published literature and derived annotations; no human-subjects or animal-subjects research was conducted by the authors. The dataset consists exclusively of curated annotations extracted from the published scientific literature, with verbatim grounding quotes attributed to their source citations.

Institutional Review Board (IRB) / Research Ethics Committee approval was therefore **not required**.

No identifiable personal data, clinical samples, animal experiments, or human genetic material were collected, generated, or processed in the course of this work. The 36,245 source documents catalogued in `source_license_manifest_v1.csv` are all peer-reviewed publications, preprints, or regulatory documents already in the public scientific record.
